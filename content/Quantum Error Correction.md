# Quantum Error Correction

Quantum error correction protects quantum information from [[Quantum Noise]] and [[Decoherence]].

It uses multiple [[Qubit|physical qubits]] to encode more reliable [[Logical Qubit|logical qubits]].

Operations such as [[CNOT Gate|CNOT]] and [[Pauli Gates|Pauli corrections]] are important components.
